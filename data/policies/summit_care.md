# Summit Care Alliance — Behavioral Health Network Policy BH-N1 (excerpt)

Out-of-network behavioral health services are not covered under standard
plans. Exception: a network-adequacy override may be granted when the
member documents that no in-network provider accepting new patients was
available within the plan's access standards (within 40 miles / 30-day
appointment availability).

Evidence accepted: dated call/contact log of at least three in-network
providers attempted, confirmation of wait times, or a payer referral
authorization.

Mental Health Parity note: visit limits and authorization requirements for
behavioral health may not be more restrictive than those applied to
medical/surgical services.
