# Atlantic Surety Health — Claims Administration Policy CA-12 (excerpt)

Participating providers must submit claims within ninety (90) calendar
days of the date of service.

A timely-filing denial will be overturned ONLY if the provider supplies
proof of original timely submission, such as:
- Electronic claim acceptance report (277CA/ERA) dated within the window
- Clearinghouse submission confirmation
- Certified-mail receipt of a paper claim

"Good cause" exceptions (natural disaster, payer system outage) require
documentary evidence. Staffing shortages, software transitions, and simple
oversight do not qualify.
