# BluePeak Benefits — Evaluation & Management Coding Policy EM-07 (excerpt)

Established patient E/M level is determined by medical decision making or
total time on the date of the encounter.

CPT 99215 (high complexity) requires either: high-level MDM (e.g., one or
more chronic illnesses with severe exacerbation/progression, or drug
therapy requiring intensive monitoring for toxicity), OR 40–54 minutes of
total time.

CPT 99213 (low complexity) is appropriate for stable chronic illness
follow-up: one stable chronic condition, minimal data review, low-risk
management (e.g., continue current medication, routine labs).

Claims billed at an unsupported level are denied (not downcoded). A
corrected claim submitted at the documented level is payable without a
formal appeal.
