# Cascade Mutual Insurance — Rehabilitation Services Policy PT-02 (excerpt)

Physical therapy is limited to twelve (12) visits per benefit year without
prior authorization. Visits 13 and beyond require pre-authorization.

Retrospective authorization MAY be granted on appeal when:
1. The ordering/rendering office can document that an authorization request
   was submitted before or promptly after the 12th visit, AND
2. Progress notes demonstrate continued skilled need — measurable,
   functional improvement consistent with an accepted rehabilitation
   protocol (e.g., post-operative protocols per surgeon's orders).

Services denied solely for "authorization absent" may be reprocessed upon
verification of a timely submitted request.
