# Meridian Health Plan — Medical Policy MP-IMG-114
## Advanced Imaging of the Spine (excerpt)

MRI of the lumbar spine (CPT 72148) is considered medically necessary when
ONE of the following is met:

1. Failure of a minimum of six (6) weeks of documented conservative
   management (e.g., physical therapy, NSAIDs, activity modification), OR
2. Presence of red-flag or progressive neurological findings, including but
   not limited to: progressive motor weakness, new or worsening reflex
   asymmetry, positive tension signs (e.g., straight-leg raise) with
   dermatomal sensory loss, cauda equina symptoms, or suspected
   fracture/malignancy/infection.

Appeals must include the treating clinician's examination findings and a
record of conservative treatment already attempted.
